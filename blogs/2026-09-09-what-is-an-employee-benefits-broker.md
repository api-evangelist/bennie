Quick answer: An employee benefits broker is a licensed professional or firm that acts as a strategic partner to help employers design, negotiate, and manage their employee benefits programs. Working on the employer's behalf, brokers go beyond shopping carriers and negotiating rates; they provide vital compliance support, mitigate risk, and align health insurance and ancillary benefits with a company's long-term business and talent goals. Brokers typically earn compensation through carrier commissions or a flat consulting fee.
