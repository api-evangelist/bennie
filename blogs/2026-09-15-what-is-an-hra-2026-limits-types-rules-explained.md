Quick answer: A Health Reimbursement Arrangement (HRA) is an employer-funded account that reimburses employees tax-free for qualified medical expenses or health insurance premiums. Unlike an FSA or HSA, the employer owns and funds an HRA entirely, and the specific rules depend on which type of HRA a company offers.
