Quick answer: A PEO (Professional Employer Organization) is a company that enters a co-employment relationship with your business, handling payroll, benefits, compliance, and HR administration on your behalf. Your employees work for your company day to day, but the PEO shares certain employer responsibilities, especially around payroll, tax filing, and benefits administration.
