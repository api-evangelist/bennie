Quick answer: A Flexible Spending Account (FSA) is a pre-tax account that lets employees set aside money from their paycheck to pay for eligible medical or dependent care expenses. For 2026, the health care FSA contribution limit is $3,400 per employee, with up to $680 allowed to carry over into the next plan year.
